Branding — three identities

Three identities for three very different clients: a premium apartment development with a mini brand book, a residential area by the forest and an electrical installation company. I worked on all three with graphic designer Roksana Mistal: we developed the concepts and chose what to present to the client together, I prepared the final artwork, and Roksana handled client communication.

Apartamenty Pod Lasem

A residential development that wanted to feel premium and close to nature. The mark joins a minimal building with a leaf: the building stands for solid foundations, the leaf for green space and an eco-friendly approach. Warm gold, a classic serif and a clean sans-serif give it a calm, upscale feel.

Mini brand book

I designed the mini brand book on my own. It sets the rules: vertical and horizontal versions with clear space (¼ and ½ of the logo height), minimum sizes of 40 and 50 mm, the main gold #B27A40 with neutral colours, black, white and grey variants, and the type pairing Lora Medium with Be Vietnam Pro.

Leśna Osiedle

Identity for a new residential area by the forest. A stylised tree built from sharp triangles stands right before the name, and warm orange meets forest green. The logo works in a stacked and a horizontal version.
