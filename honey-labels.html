Pasieka św. Ambrożego — honey labels

Labels for St. Ambrose Apiary (Pasieka św. Ambrożego), which launches a new honey every year. I designed their flavoured honey line from scratch and I add new flavours to both of their collections.

The brief

Every year the apiary launches a new honey: natural, or blended with fruit, herbs or flowers. Each one needs its own label, yet on the shelf all the jars must read instantly as one brand.

Key decision

Together with the owner we chose eco kraft paper and vintage botanical illustrations, and I built the label around a diamond: the ingredients frame the flavour name from the top and bottom corners. The system has worked for years: a new flavour means new illustrations, not a new design.
