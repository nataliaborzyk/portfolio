Product & surface design · Vintage Murals

Decals that break out of the frame.

Circle and arch-shaped 3D wall decals for Vintage Murals, a Polish wall décor producer, sold in their Etsy stores. The description refers to my time on the project; the stores and listings are now run by the company.

[Hero: the best decal on a real wall, wide shot with furniture for scale]

Client
Vintage Murals
My role
concept, collage, cut files
Designs
about 30
Where
Etsy stores

The challenge

Many customers loved murals but did not want to pay for, or commit to, a whole wall of wallpaper. The brand needed a smaller product with the same visual impact.

The idea

Circle and arch-shaped decals where petals, leaves and ornaments step outside the shape. On the wall it reads as if the image is coming out of its frame.

Process

Collages built from public-domain artworks. I combined flowers and leaves from different sources, let some of them break out of the shape, and prepared cut files for a Summa plotter.

[Close-up: a petal edge crossing the frame]

[Arch variant on a wall]

The result

Customers welcomed the decals as an affordable statement piece: the effect of a mural for those who didn’t want, or couldn’t afford, a whole wall of wallpaper.
