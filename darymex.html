Darymex — labels & newsletters

bedspread labels

A refreshed collection of labels for the bedspread range, designed together with graphic designer Edyta Wojaczek. The same products went to hotel chains, large retailers and Allegro, so the labels had to inform clearly and look good both on a shelf and in product photos. Each label pairs a handwritten collection name with a clear information strip: composition, care symbols and the list of available sizes.

newsletters

Templates for the e-mails the online store sent automatically: a welcome message, a reminder about products left in the basket and a discount on products the customer had viewed. I designed the layouts and wrote the copy together with a copywriter; sending and automation were handled by someone else. Every e-mail ends with the same blocks – the store's benefits shown as icons, products other customers are buying and contact details – so the whole series feels consistent.
