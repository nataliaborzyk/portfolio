The challenge

Wall décor is one of the most crowded categories on Etsy. A printing house wanted to sell its wallpapers and murals internationally, and one generic shop would have disappeared among thousands of others.

The approach

I researched competitors and bestsellers to find gaps in the market. One of them was panoramic wallpaper designed to wrap a whole room, which found a strong following among fans of Victorian-style interiors and became one of the top-performing stores. For each of the five storefronts I created the name, logo and visual identity, and designed every listing.

Source material

The murals were built from public-domain artworks and stock licensed by the company, not from my own illustrations. My work was everything that turned an old painting into a product: retouching and restoration, upscaling for large-format print, rearranging elements (moving a bird to the other side, adding a branch from another artwork), building collages and changing colour palettes.

Custom orders

Custom orders came from private customers and from businesses: hotels, restaurants and schools. Besides adapting the design to the wall, I recommended the right material for each space: certified fire-retardant materials for hotels and schools, where safety came first, and washable finishes for restaurants. The range included non-woven, vinyl, self-adhesive and textured self-adhesive wallpaper imitating canvas or brushstrokes, which made printed paintings look especially convincing.

The result

Together the stores generated over 9,000 sales. Several designs received Etsy's Pick status, and I handled customer service for all five stores myself.
