✱ The challenge

Wall décor is one of the most crowded categories on Etsy. A printing house wanted to sell its wallpapers and murals internationally, and one generic shop would have disappeared among thousands of others.

✱ The approach

I researched competitors and bestsellers to find gaps in the market, then built a separate brand for each niche. One of them was panoramic wallpaper designed to wrap a whole room, which found a strong following among fans of Victorian-style interiors and became one of the top-performing stores.

Five brands, five niches

Each store has its own name, identity and visual style, built for a different customer.
